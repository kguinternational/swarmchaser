On behalf of the **JC Coffey Foundation** (`501(c)(3) Public Charity, EIN 33-3450194`), I am writing to formally request an in-kind donation of off-lease or surplus computing hardware—specifically **Apple Mac Studio, Mac Mini, or high-performance GPU workstations**—to power our free Youth Robotics, Open-Source Ecological Engineering, and Digital Literacy laboratory in North Carolina.

### About Our Foundation & Active Programs
The JC Coffey Foundation operates under our founding covenant, *No Human Left Behind*, delivering community empowerment, hands-on STEM education, and ecological remediation in technical partnership with KGU International. 

Our active institutional initiatives include:
1. **`A¹ Community Action`:** Direct crisis relief, resource mobilization, and our flagship **Help Wyatt** campaign delivering aid to families facing acute developmental and medical challenges.
2. **`B¹ Educational Outreach`:** 100% tuition-free STEM workshops teaching rural youth physical robotics (OpenBot), 3D CAD modeling (FreeCAD), circuit design (KiCad), and autonomous drone aviation.
3. **`C¹ Sovereign Alliance`:** Deploying off-grid **Sovereign Field Nodes**, clean water biochar reactors, and self-funding solar mesh Wi-Fi networks in disaster-prone rural areas.

### The Specific Hardware Need: Why Unified Memory & GPU Acceleration?
To teach advanced computer vision, mechanical CAD simulation, and edge intelligence without requiring low-income students to pay expensive cloud computing subscriptions, our lab relies on on-premise hardware:
* **Apple Silicon (Mac Studio & Mac Mini):** Massive unified memory allows students to run local engineering models, CAD assemblies, and zero-egress AI without cloud latency or data harvesting.
* **NVIDIA Workstations & Edge Modules:** CUDA and TensorRT acceleration for real-time drone computer vision and thermal search-and-rescue feeds.
