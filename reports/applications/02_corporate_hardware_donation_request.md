#### About Our Foundation & Mission
The JC Coffey Foundation operates under our founding covenant, *No Human Left Behind*, delivering community empowerment, hands-on STEM education, and ecological remediation in technical partnership with KGU International. 

Our programs provide 100% tuition-free educational training to under-resourced youth, teaching them:
1. **Physical Robotics:** Designing, 3D printing, and programming low-cost autonomous rovers using the open-source **OpenBot** platform.
2. **Ecological Engineering:** Modeling clean water filtration (gravity-fed biochar systems) and off-grid solar microgrids using open-source simulation software (FreeCAD, KiCad, and OpenFOAM).
3. **Decentralized Computing:** Deploying local, zero-egress computing infrastructure that protects student data and privacy without commercial cloud exploitation.

#### The Specific Hardware Need: Why Apple Silicon / Mac Studio?
To teach advanced computer vision, mechanical CAD simulation, and edge intelligence without requiring students to pay expensive cloud computing subscriptions, our lab relies on the unified memory architecture of Apple Silicon (M-series processors). 

A single donated **Mac Studio workstation** enables us to:
* Run local, air-gapped engineering simulations (CAD stress analysis and fluid dynamics).
* Serve as the master compile-and-flash station for dozens of student OpenBot rovers.
* Provide high-performance digital workstations for youth who have no computer access at home.
