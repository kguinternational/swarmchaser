### Field 3: Project Summary (150 Words)
The JC Coffey Foundation (`501(c)(3) EIN 33-3450194`), operating under our founding covenant *No Human Left Behind*, delivers hands-on digital equity, youth robotics education, and community resilience in partnership with KGU International. 

This project establishes a local community technology hub in North Carolina focused on two urgent community needs:
1. **Community Wi-Fi Field Nodes:** Deploying weather-sealed, solar-assisted mesh Wi-Fi nodes to provide reliable internet connectivity for under-resourced students and rural families.
2. **OpenBot STEM Robotics Workshops:** Providing hands-on education where youth learn 3D CAD modeling, embedded microcontrollers, and autonomous robotics to prepare for high-wage technology careers.

Funding from Walmart Spark Good will directly purchase hardware, field wiring, solar storage enclosures, and workstation equipment to empower local students with free digital access and practical engineering skills.

---

### Field 4: Community Need & Target Population (250 Words)
Access to reliable high-speed connectivity and modern computing hardware remains one of the largest socioeconomic dividers facing students and families in our service area. Disadvantaged youth frequently lack the computing workstations and hands-on laboratory equipment needed to learn coding, computer-aided design (CAD), and robotics.

Furthermore, during severe weather disruptions or localized grid outages, vulnerable households lose critical communication channels. Our Sovereign Field Node initiative deploys decentralized, off-grid mesh Wi-Fi access points that maintain community connectivity for schoolwork, emergency notices, and telehealth.

Through the KGU Youth Resilience Scholarship program, we eliminate financial barriers by providing 100% tuition-free access to our digital workshops. We specifically target students underrepresented in STEM fields, pairing them with hands-on hardware kits (OpenBot) that turn abstract code into working physical machines.
