### 1. Who We Are & What We Do in North Carolina
The **JC Coffey Foundation**, operating under *No Human Left Behind*, provides 100% tuition-free STEM and disaster resilience education to under-resourced rural youth in North Carolina. 

Our students learn **Emergency Preparedness & Technological Self-Sustainability** by physically building, 3D printing, programming, and deploying:
1. **Autonomous Aerial Drones & Thermal Cameras:** Rapid post-hurricane flood mapping and search-and-rescue.
2. **Ground Field Rovers (OpenBot):** 3D-printed agricultural rovers with onboard edge AI vision.
3. **Off-Grid Solar Microgrids & Clean Water Reactors:** Gravity-fed water purifiers removing flood toxins.
