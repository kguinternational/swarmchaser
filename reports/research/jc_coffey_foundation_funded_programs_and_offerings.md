## 1. Executive Overview & Statutory Purpose

The **JC Coffey Foundation** operates as a non-traditional, community-embedded public charity dedicated to democratizing sovereign technology, open-source engineering, ecological resilience, and humanitarian relief. Rather than maintaining heavy bureaucratic overhead, **100% of all public donations, grants, and corporate sponsorships** flow directly to program execution, governed by our statutory charter:
* **70% Direct Physical Remediation & Equipment Pool:** Acquiring and maintaining physical field nodes, water filtration reactors, solar microgrids, autonomous rovers, and community broadband hardware.
* **30% KGU Youth Resilience Scholarships:** Providing 100% tuition-free engineering education, spatial learning quests, and direct youth stipends.

## 2. Core Operational Pillars & Funded Offerings

The Foundation organizes its primary work across three foundational pillars and dedicated feasibility tracks:

### Pillar 1: `A¹ Community Action` (Direct Aid & Offline Relief)
* **Program Focus:** Direct humanitarian assistance, physical resource distribution, and crisis response.
* **Technology Layer:** Utilizes **NHIL Key credentials** for secure, proof-gated, private communication and offline-verified aid delivery.
* **Impact:** Delivers food, emergency water, and power equipment to rural and distressed households during extreme weather and economic displacement without harvesting invasive personal data.

### Pillar 2: `B¹ Educational Outreach` (Spatial Quests & Digital Literacy)
* **Program Focus:** Place-based, hands-on STEM education for youth who lack access to advanced computing.
* **Pedagogy:** Instead of conventional lectures, students participate in interactive physical challenges and spatial quests driven by **DTOS Spatial Mode**.
* **Offerings:**
  - 3D CAD design and additive fabrication (FreeCAD + 3D printers).
  - Physical electronics and circuit layout (KiCad).
  - OpenBot educational robotics assembly and microcontroller flashing.
  - Zero-cost community workshops preparing youth for engineering careers.

### Pillar 3: `C¹ Sovereign Alliance` (Infrastructure & Field Nodes)
* **Program Focus:** Establishing off-grid, disaster-resilient community utilities in technical partnership with **KGU International**.
* **Infrastructure:** Deploying **Sovereign Field Nodes** equipped with local **CoRe AI intelligence**, solar power, and mesh communications that continue operating when municipal power and internet grids fail.
